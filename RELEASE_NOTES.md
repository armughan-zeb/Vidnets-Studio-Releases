# Vidnets Studio 1.0.3

- Lifetime Pro is the only public plan: $149 once at launch, plus applicable tax. Provider usage is separate.
- Monthly/yearly products are closed to new sales. Existing paid subscriptions and lifetime access are preserved.
- Private trials can only be granted by the owner in the admin panel. Downloading and signing in do not grant trial access.
- Purchase Lifetime Pro buttons on the sign-in screen and locked studio open the website's secure purchase flow. Use the same Google account for purchase and app sign-in; refresh access after payment.
- Owner-granted trials retain five exports per UTC calendar month, up to one minute each. A trial cannot replace an active paid assignment.
- Generation access, export quotas and device rules remain enforced by Supabase. No admin credentials are included in customer packages.

Windows 10/11 x64. Bundled rendering runtime. External provider accounts and internet are needed for generation. The installer is unsigned; verify its SHA-256 against `checksums.txt`.
